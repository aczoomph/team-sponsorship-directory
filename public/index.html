Basis

Every entry comes from public announcements and press coverage, with links to the sources. Reported deal values and terms are the figures the cited outlet published. They are not Zoomph valuations and have not been verified, so cite the outlet if you repeat one.

What's in it

Game jersey sponsors for the NBA (from the start of the patch program in 2017-18), the NBA G League (from 2023-24) and the NHL (from the start of its patch program in 2022-23), through the current or next announced season. NHL helmet decal sponsors are listed from 2025-26 as a separate section, NHL (Helmet Decal). Many NHL teams have different home and road helmet sponsors; the road one is listed as a secondary helmet decal. A decal that started before 2025-26 keeps its real start season where a source gave it. The WNBA is next. G League coverage online is thin, so more G League entries are medium or low confidence, and "No jersey sponsor found" means none could be confirmed, not that the team definitely has none. Where a team carries two jersey sponsors at once (common in the NHL, which often has different home and road patches), the second is listed as a secondary jersey patch. The NHL season was already under way when the NHL was added, so a new 2026-27 NHL patch shows as current and "New for 2026-27" rather than "Announced, not yet worn". The directory is built to hold any sponsorship type (practice jerseys, helmets, naming rights and so on) and more leagues will be added.

Status

  • Current: the sponsor on the team's jerseys or helmets now, or continuing into the next season.
  • Announced, not yet worn: a deal that has been announced but hasn't appeared in a game yet.
  • Past: the deal has ended. The seasons it ran are kept so the history stays complete.

Confidence

  • High: an official team or league release, or two reputable outlets agree.
  • Medium: one reputable outlet, or one detail (usually the end season) is inferred. The notes say which.
  • Low: sources are unclear or conflict. Check the notes before relying on it.

How it stays current

Once a day Claude checks sports business news for new jersey patch and NHL helmet decal announcements. Each one it finds becomes a proposed change, with sources, and Andrew approves or rejects each one. Approved changes are then published to this site, so the site can be a day or two behind an announcement. The "Data as of" date at the top says when it was last updated. The check only reads public news. It never changes anything in the Zoomph platform.
